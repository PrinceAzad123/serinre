Our Story

From Leaf to Cup,
With Love

Founded in 2020, Sereni-Tea was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves a perfect cup of tea. We travel the world to find the finest leaves, working directly with small-scale farmers who share our passion for quality.

Our AI-powered platform learns your preferences over time, creating a personalized tea journey that evolves with your palate. Whether you're a seasoned connoisseur or just starting your tea adventure, we're here to guide you.
